About

I am a PhD candidate in Comparative Literature at the University of Amsterdam (Amsterdam School for Cultural Analysis & Amsterdam Institute for Humanities Research). I hold an MA in English Studies (Distinction) from the University of Hong Kong and a BA in Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages from Beijing Language and Culture University, and spent an exchange year at the School of Modern Languages, Newcastle University.

My research is located at the intersection of modernist studies, Chinese modernity, and word/image studies. My dissertation, Modern Self and Modern Horror: Post-Impressionist Aesthetics in Virginia Woolf and Eileen Chang, examines how colour, visuality, and the painterly gaze shape modern selfhood and its anxieties across Anglophone and Chinese literary traditions. Related work has been published or is forthcoming in The Conradian and in edited collections including The Politics of Intermedial Modernism (ed. Elicia Clements) and the selected papers of the 33rd Annual International Conference on Virginia Woolf. I have presented at the International Virginia Woolf Society, IAWIS/AIERTI, and the Global Digital Humanities Symposium, among others. Alongside close reading, I work with digital humanities methods, including stylometric analysis in R and interactive narrative in Twine, and I organise workshops where literary texts become participatory, collaborative objects.

Beyond the page, I work in practice-based and curatorial settings: I assisted artist Sixin Zeng with the installation of The 2.5D Nomad at Prospects 2026 (Mondriaan Fund, Art Rotterdam), and I created Journey to Conlay, a playable digital story based on Joseph Conrad’s travel writings. I also serve as a seminar organiser at ASCA and as an assistant for the IAWIS/AIERTI 2026 conference in Amsterdam.

Research Interests: modernism; Chinese modernity; Virginia Woolf and Eileen Chang; post-impressionist aesthetics; word and image studies; feminist aesthetics and female writing; comparative literature; digital humanities and interactive digital narrative.

Art & Digital Projects

Exhibition Assistant · 2026

The 2.5D Nomad by Sixin Zeng

Prospects 2026, Mondriaan Fund, within Art Rotterdam, Ahoy, Rotterdam, 27–29 March 2026

I assisted the artist with the installation and presentation of The 2.5D Nomad (2025–ongoing), a multimedia work of video, sculpture, mirrored surfaces and sound (headphones interwoven with an anime wig) that documents Chinese cosplayers in Europe and explores migration, identity formation and the in-between space of the Asian diaspora. I also supported exhibition logistics and on-site presentation at Prospects 2026 (92 participating artists; curated by Johan Gustavsson and Daphne Verberg), and continue to take part in the project as an exhibition and production assistant.
